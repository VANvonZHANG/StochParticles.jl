Thermodynamics

StochParticles.jl provides a complete thermodynamics module for Köhler theory and vapor pressure equilibrium calculations used in condensation simulations.

Parameters

StochParticles.ThermodynamicsParams — Type
ThermodynamicsParams

Physical parameters for H2O thermodynamics calculations.

Fields

  • κ_values::SVector{A,Float64} — hygroscopicity κ per species
  • σ::Float64 — surface tension [N/m]
  • ρ_w::Float64 — water density [kg/m³]
  • M_w::Float64 — water molar mass [kg/mol]
  • L_v::Float64 — latent heat of vaporization [J/kg]
  • R_v::Float64 — specific gas constant for water vapor [J/kg/K]
  • D_v::Float64 — molecular diffusivity of water vapor [m²/s]
  • k_a::Float64 — thermal conductivity of air [W/m/K]

Vapor Pressure

StochParticles.saturation_vapor_pressure — Function
saturation_vapor_pressure(T::Real) -> Real

Saturation vapor pressure over liquid water, Buck (1981) formulation:

e_s = 611.21 · exp((18.678 − T_c/234.5) · T_c / (257.14 + T_c))

Accuracy ±0.1% against Smithsonian/Goff-Gratch tables over cloud-physics temperatures (replaces the constant-L_v Clausius-Clapeyron form, which ran −0.6% low at 285 K — audit note 2026-10-08).

equilibrium_vapor_pressure(m_dry, m_w, thermo, densities, T) -> Float64

Köhler equilibrium vapor pressure over a solution droplet.

peq = psat(T) · aw · exp(2σ / (Rv · T · ρ_w · R))

Arguments

  • m_dry — dry species masses
  • m_w — water mass
  • thermo — ThermodynamicsParams
  • densities — per-species densities
  • T — temperature [K]
StochParticles.modified_diffusion_coefficient — Function
modified_diffusion_coefficient(thermo, T, p_sat) -> Real

Effective vapor diffusivity coupling molecular diffusion with latent-heat conduction (Mason 1971; Pruppacher & Klett; Seinfeld & Pandis Eq. 13.58; Ghan 2011 Eq. A1 recast from G-form to D′-form):

1/D_v' = 1/D_v + (L_v/k_a)·(dρ_sat/dT),
dρ_sat/dT = (ρ_sat/T)·(L_v·M_w/(R_u·T) − 1)      [exact Clausius-Clapeyron]

with ρsat = psat·Mw/(Ru·T) and Ru = Rv·Mw. Term-by-term equivalent to the Ghan(2011)/pyrcel growth coefficient: multiplying the resistances by ρw·R·T/(es·Mw) maps 1/Dv → Ga and the thermal term → Gb = (Lv·ρw/(ka·T))·(Lv·Mw/(R·T) − 1). The thermal resistance reduces Dv' by a factor ≈3-4 at cloud temperatures (293.15 K: Dv' ≈ 0.27·D_v with library constants).

Transition-regime note: this is the R → ∞ (continuum) limit. The radius-dependent form with Ghan 2011's SPLIT corrections (vapor branch with condensation coefficient ac, heat branch with thermal accommodation aT — Eqs. A2-A3) is the 5-argument method below; the H2O flux calls that one. At ac = aT = 1 the split vs a single vapor-side factor on the combined Dv' agree to ≲3% (ℓv ≈ 0.17 µm vs ℓt ≈ 0.19 µm at 285 K), but the split is required for ac ≠ 1 sensitivity studies.

Audit note 2026-10-13: the previous assembly carried a spurious p_sat/p_atm factor (patm hardwired to 101325 Pa) in the thermal term, shrinking the thermal resistance ~40× and making the condensation sink 2.5-2.8× too strong — the root cause of the Smax 2× offset vs pyrcel found in M4 leg 1. The form above is assembled from first principles; the −1 in dρsat/dT is kept exact (dropping it overstates the thermal resistance by Lv·Mw/(R·T)/(Lv·Mw/(R·T)−1) ≈ 5.6% at 285 K). Library constants are kept (Buck es, T-dependent Lv); pyrcel's Lv = 2.25e6 J/kg is 9% low and is NOT adopted (M3 audit 2026-10-08).

modified_diffusion_coefficient(thermo, T, p_sat, R, p_air;
                               accom_c = 1.0, accom_T = 1.0) -> Real

Full Ghan (2011) assembly with SPLIT transition-regime corrections (Seinfeld & Pandis / Duan et al. 2018 Eqs. A1-A3; the pyrcel form):

1/D̃' = (1 + ℓ_v/(a_c·R))/D_v + (1 + ℓ_t/(a_T·R))·(L_v/k_a)·(dρ_sat/dT)

Each resistance branch carries its own kinetic correction — vapor transport with the mass condensation coefficient a_c (correction length ℓ_v = D_v·√(2π·M_w/(R_u·T))), heat conduction with the thermal accommodation coefficient a_T (ℓ_t = (k_a/(ρ_a·c_p))·√(2π·M_a/(R_u·T)), air density ρ_a = p_air/(R_d·T)). The 3-argument form is the R → ∞ (continuum) limit of this one. At a_c = a_T = 1 the split and the legacy single-factor form agree to ≲3% (ℓv ≈ 0.17 µm vs ℓt ≈ 0.19 µm at 285 K); for a_c ≠ 1 sensitivity studies the split is required — applying one vapor-side factor to the combined D′ wrongly scales the thermal branch too.

Köhler Theory

StochParticles.water_activity — Function
water_activity(m_dry, m_w, κ_values, densities) -> Float64

Compute water activity using κ-Köhler theory (Petters & Kreidenweis, 2007).

Arguments

  • m_dry::SVector{A} — dry mass of each species [kg]
  • m_w — water mass [kg]
  • κ_values::SVector{A} — hygroscopicity parameter κ for each species
  • densities::SVector{A} — density of each species [kg/m³]

Returns

  • Water activity a_w ∈ [0, 1]

Formula

a_w = V_w / (V_w + V_dry · κ_mix)

where Vw = mw / ρw, Vdry = Σ mk / ρk, and κmix = Σ εk · κk with εk = Vk / Vdry.

StochParticles.critical_supersaturation — Function
critical_supersaturation(m_dry, thermo, densities, T) -> Float64

Thin wrapper over critical_point returning only Sc. Behavior identical to pre-M3 (same search, same values) — existing tests are the regression gate.

Arguments

  • m_dry::SVector{A} — dry species masses [kg]
  • thermo::ThermodynamicsParams — thermodynamic parameters
  • densities::SVector{A} — per-species densities [kg/m³]
  • T::Float64 — temperature [K]

Returns

  • Critical supersaturation Sc (dimensionless, e.g., 0.0015 = 0.15%)

Reference

Petters & Kreidenweis (2007), ACP. Numerical maximum of exact Köhler curve replaces the approximate analytical formula.
